// 01 · About

Hello, I'm Vidhi.

I'm a Data Scientist at Infoblox working on applied ML and AI systems for cybersecurity infrastructure — spanning anomaly detection, LLM fine-tuning, agentic AI for on-call triage, and security log analytics.

I hold an M.S. in Computer Science from Northwestern University (4.0 GPA) and a dual B.Tech + MBA from NMIMS Mumbai. My research background covers cybersecurity, healthcare AI, and computer vision, published across IEEE, Springer, Elsevier, and IET venues.

Outside work, I write about AI and ML on my tech journal, and I'm endlessly curious about the systems being built at the frontier.
